What road types should be available?

Select all that apply — these become the palette of road tools

A

Residential / Local Street

Narrow, low-speed. Two lanes, no center line. The fabric of neighborhoods.

~10m wide
B

Collector / Boulevard

Medium width with a center stripe or median. Connects neighborhoods to arterials.

~18m wide
C

Arterial

Multi-lane road with a median. Major city roads, 4–6 lanes total.

~30m wide
D

Highway / Expressway

High-speed, grade-separated or limited access. Heavy visual footprint.

~50m wide
E

Protected Bike Lane / Path

Dedicated cycling infrastructure, often alongside other roads or standalone.

~4m wide
F

Pedestrian Path / Walkway

Foot-traffic only. Parks, plazas, greenways.

~3m wide
