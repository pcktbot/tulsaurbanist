How should the road disruption zone look?

The area beneath a placed road — communicating "this space has been reworked"

Frosted Glass

A blur + light wash over the map tiles. Clean, architectural feel.

Construction Hatch

Diagonal stripe pattern — communicates active demolition / work zone.

Dark Shadow

Map dims beneath the road zone. Understated, keeps road prominent.

Bleached / Washed Out

Map fades to near-white in the disruption zone. Feels like cleared land.
